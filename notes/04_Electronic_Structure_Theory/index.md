The core of the notebook, arranged as a textbook. Chapters 1-7 build the mean-field picture: the Hamiltonian, determinants, Hartree-Fock, basis sets, second quantization, density matrices and orbital optimization. Chapters 9-14 add electron correlation: CI, perturbation theory, coupled cluster, multiconfigurational methods, and how they compare. Chapters 15-17 cover properties: analytic derivatives, geometries and propagators. Chapter 19 covers quantum embedding (DMET, up to its analytic gradient). Chapters 18 and 20-22 are planned.

**How to read it.** Read the chapters in order; the Previous/Next buttons follow the same order. Use two passes. The first pass takes only the notes marked *core* in each chapter introduction, at the level of Szabo-Ostlund or Jørgensen-Simons: first quantization, explicit determinants, small worked models. The second pass adds the *deep* notes, at the level of Helgaker-Jørgensen-Olsen: second quantization, exponential parametrizations, Lagrangians and production algorithms. Chapters 5, 6, 7, 12, 15 and 17 are second-pass chapters, and Chapter 5 is the prerequisite for almost every deep note. Each chapter ends its reading list with a checkpoint: a derivation or number to reproduce before moving on. Most of the worked numbers already sit inside the notes, so the test is to rederive them before looking.
