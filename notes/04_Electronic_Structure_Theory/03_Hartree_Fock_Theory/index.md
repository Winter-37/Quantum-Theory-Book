The mean-field approximation, derived and solved.

**Path.**

1. The Fock operator, the Hartree-Fock equations by constrained minimization, and two consequences: Brillouin's theorem and Koopmans' theorem.
2. Restricted HF in a basis (the Roothaan equations), orthogonalization of the basis, and the SCF procedure, worked through for HeH$^+$.
3. Population analysis.
4. Unrestricted HF and the H$_2$ dissociation problem, which motivates everything in Chapters 9-13.
5. Generalized HF, and the classification of HF solutions by the symmetries they keep.

**Leads to.** Chapter 4 (basis sets) and Chapter 7 (HF as an optimization problem).

**Planned sections:** Restricted open-shell Hartree-Fock (ROHF); Maximum-overlap method for excited-state SCF; Initial guesses.
