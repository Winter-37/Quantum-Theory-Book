Electronic-structure methods on quantum computers. The chapters written so far take the shortest path from second quantization to the methods used in the owner's research: map the Hamiltonian to qubits (Chapter 2), estimate energies variationally (Chapter 3), sample determinants instead (Chapter 5), and embed both in DMET (Chapter 7). Part 5 (qubits, gates, circuits) is still planned, so these notes define the little quantum-computing language they need as they go.
