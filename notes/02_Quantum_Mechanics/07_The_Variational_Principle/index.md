Every wave-function method in Part 4 is either variational or measured against a variational bound, so this chapter is the entry point to electronic-structure theory.

**Path.** The variational principle, then how it is used to rank and improve trial functions. Linear variation turns the problem into a matrix eigenvalue problem (the secular equation behind Hartree-Fock and CI). Hylleraas-Undheim interlacing shows that excited roots are bounds too.
