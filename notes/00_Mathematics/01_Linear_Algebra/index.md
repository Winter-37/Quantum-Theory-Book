Electronic-structure theory is mostly linear algebra in disguise. Hartree-Fock and CI are matrix eigenvalue problems, orbital rotations are matrix exponentials, and coupled cluster rests on a commutator expansion.

**Path.** Matrices and determinants, then Dirac notation and changes of basis. The Hermitian eigenvalue problem comes next, then functions of matrices ($\mathbf S^{-1/2}$, $e^{\mathbf A}$). The Baker-Campbell-Hausdorff expansion and the exponential parametrization of unitary matrices follow. The chapter ends by carrying all of this over from vectors to function spaces.

**Leads to.** Part 2 (operators and observables) and Part 4, Chapters 3, 5 and 7.

**Planned sections:** Singular value decomposition and low-rank approximation; Iterative diagonalization (Davidson, Lanczos).
